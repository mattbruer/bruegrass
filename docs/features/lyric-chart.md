# The lyric chart

- **Lyric chart** (`/arranger/chart`, logic `lyricChart/`, UI `components/lyricChart/`): the song as a chart for the band to read and print: title, key/capo/tempo/time, words under the bars, chords only where they change (% for a bar with nothing new), section names, double barline at a section's end, the playing bar lit while the band plays (`followPlayback.js`). Words are lines under a section line in the arrangement text (`chart/sectionLyrics.js`; `|` starts the next bar, words before the first `|` are a pickup, each lyric line is a row); the chord chart ignores them, and both read sections through `chart/sectionBlocks.js`. Bars come from `chart/chartBars.js` (shared with the scrolling strip and the help page). It's drawn on paper by the generic `$lib/components/paper/` (`PaperPages`: blocks measured off screen, paginated, scaled to fit between the top bar and the player bar, side by side as many as fit, swipe/arrows for the rest, one sheet per printed page; US Letter with 0.4 in margins in `paper.js`). **The paper is always white with black text, whatever the theme** (it resets the ink and accent variables on `.sheet`). **The chart fills the page**: `chooseTextScale.js` finds the largest text scale at which the whole song fits one page, in one column (`COLUMN_COUNTS = [1]`: two columns looked lame, so they're off, but `columnLayout.js` still handles them: add 2 to the list), falling back to several pages, as large as the page is wide, when it can't fit one page at 0.5 or more; spare height goes between the rows (`extraGap`, capped). Blocks are measured at each trial scale off screen (`--bars-wrap: nowrap` there and on the sheet, so a too-wide row shows as overflow instead of wrapping); the sheet draws each block in a `scale()` wrapper. The chart page's A−/Fit/A+ (`TextSizeControl.svelte`, `settings.chart.textSize`, 0 = Fit) steps from the scale in use and never goes wider than the page. Words in a bar sit on equal columns from the barline (`ChartBar.svelte`); the idea not built is placing each syllable at its note's beat when a melody exists (the words-under-the-melody matching in `melody/words/`), with equal columns as the fallback. Checked: short songs one and two pages, phone and desktop; check a really long song (rows are four bars when they have no words; eight to a row would be the next thing to try); printing not tried. The chart's own blocks are `lyricChart/chartBlocks.js`; `LyricChart.svelte` draws the same blocks without paper (help page). Full screen (`$lib/components/FullScreen.svelte`, generic: covers the page, and hides the browser's toolbars where allowed; not on iPhone) turns pages with a tap on either third, arrows, Page Up/Down or space (`paper/turnPages.js`: page-turner pedals), with Play/Stop in its corner.
