# The arranger's pages

- **The arranger's pages share one song**: `src/routes/arranger/(band)/+layout.svelte` sets up the arranger (state, drafts, guards, player bar, drawers) for the pages inside it, `/arranger`, `/arranger/chart` and `/arranger/tracks`, so moving between them keeps the song and the music (nothing asks when leaving any page: songs save themselves). Help (`/arranger/help`) stays outside: it opens in its own tab. Every page has the tune without drawing it: it's read in state (see "The song is read once" below).
- **The song's key** is one decision (`chart/key.js` `songKey`): the chart's `[K:G]` before the first bar (`chart/keyField.js`, which only ever reads or writes that one, so naming the song's key leaves later key changes alone; the song's own key, as it sounds before any Key change) or else the first chord. Key changes later in the song are their own `[K:]`s (below, Arrangement text). The strip, chord labels, Key menu, harmony and the melody all read it; the melody's K: is it (`melody/roll/melodyKey.js`). Naming it in the piano roll's Key menu (`Arranger.nameSongKey`) writes `[K:]` and respells every voice of the ABC without moving a pitch (hand-edited harmony stays).
- **Words under the melody**: the Melody tab's "Add the chart's words to the melody" (`melody/words/`: `syllablesOf` splits at spaces and hyphens, `placeInBar` puts a bar's own syllables on its first notes and the next section's pickup on its last, `abcWithWords` writes ABC `w:` lines, one per pass through repeated music, replacing old ones under the melody only). abcjs skips rests but gives a tied-into note a syllable, so those get `_`. The report shows under the buttons (`arranger.conversionReport`, shared with the chord conversions). Words on the scrolling strip were tried and taken off for now; to bring them back, pass the strip `readLyrics(text)` and show `wordsInBar` (it follows a loop region's `source` section) under each bar.
- The page `src/routes/arranger/(band)/+page.svelte` is only an outline. The music fills the page, one view at a time: the chords scrolling past (the default: every visit starts on it, the user's call), the piano roll (`MelodyDrawing`, to draw and edit the tune right on the page) or the sheet music (a Chords | Piano roll | Sheet music switch on the player card, `components/page/musicView.js`; the choice lives on `arranger.layout.musicView`, so it holds between the band pages but isn't saved; Sheet music only with a tune). The player card keeps capo, key and loop in both (the old /arranger page, being phased out; on Tracks the Capo is in the Guitar lane's menu and the Key in the Band lane's); a player bar is fixed to the bottom of the screen on phone and desktop alike (`components/page/PlayerBar.svelte`), except on pages listed in `components/page/bandPages.js` (Learn), and the editor (Chords | Melody tabs) and mixer slide up above it, one at a time (`components/page/BottomDrawer.svelte`, `arranger.layout.drawer`). The phone keypad covers the bar while it's up, with the drawer on top of it. A new drawer is a `BottomDrawer` plus a `DrawerButton`.
- **Pages use the whole width**: the arranger's pages, the top bar, the player bar and the drawers share one width, `ui-page-width` in `theme/ui.css` (a small gutter, capped at 120rem). The sheet music keeps notes at about printed size and fits more bars on a line as the page widens (`staffLayout` in `melody/drawSheetMusic.js`, `STAFF_WIDTH_PER_BAR`), reflowing when the window's width changes.
- **A chord is named only where it changes** (the user's call), on the scrolling strip and in the sheet music: `|G|G|C|` reads G, –, C, plus the first chord of every section. Display only: the chart text and the ABC keep every chord as written (`chart/chordChanges.js` `namesChord` for the strip, `melody/chordChangesOnPaper.js` for the sheet music, per voice, again after each `P:` line). The lyric chart already did this its own way (% for a bar with nothing new).
- **The scrolling strip rests on the last edit**: while stopped, `ArrangementStrip` slides to the first bar whose chords the last edit changed (it shows only a few bars at a time), and back to bar 1 when Play starts.
- The arranger opens on the last song this device had open, else the starter song in `src/lib/arranger/starterSong.js` (Amazing Grace, written for this app, with its words in the chart and under the melody; Keep on the Sunny Side, in the "New from…" menu with the other examples and "A recording…", is a verse-and-chorus song with words but no melody yet); New asks for a title, the key and the time first (`components/song/NewSongDialog.svelte`, no way round it: the key you name is the one truth Key and capo move from; the times are the open song's style's, and the new song keeps that style), starts `newSong({ key, time, style })` from the same file and names it: `chart/newChart.js` writes `"Verse" [M:4/4] [K:G] |G|N.C.|…|`, 16 bars, the key's chord in bar 1 and N.C. (nothing played) to write over.
- Don't take ABC from thesession.org: its content licence forbids use with LLMs. Write public-domain tunes ourselves.
