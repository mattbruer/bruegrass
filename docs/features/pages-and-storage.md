# Pages, top bar and browser storage

- **The player card is a plain `ui-card`** like the rest of the app (`components/player/PlayerCard.svelte`). The vintage looks (walnut, tape deck) were tried and dropped: the user wants a typical web app look, not something pretending to be real hardware. The chart strip scrolls behind a dark bezel under `chart/GlassPlayhead.svelte`'s red-tinted glass line.
- Every page: the top bar (`src/lib/components/TopBar.svelte`) and the Settings panel (`src/lib/settings/`), set up in `src/routes/+layout.svelte`. **Nothing may be wider than a phone** (375 px): a page wider than the screen makes every dialog and drawer size to it and get cut off. The top bar drops its wordmark below 640 px so its buttons fit; title rows with links beside them wrap (`flex-wrap`). Check with `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` in the pane's mobile preset.
- **Stored on this device** (`/storage`, the disks icon in the top bar; `src/lib/browserStorage/`): shows everything the app keeps in this browser as it actually is (localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB databases and their records with audio summarised, caches, space used and whether the browser protects it) and deletes any one or everything. **When adding a storage key or database, add its plain-words entry to `browserStorage/knownStorage.js`.** Databases are opened through `keptOpen` (`storage/indexedDb.js`), which lets go when the page deletes them. Deleting a song deletes its takes (`deleteSongTakes`).
